Privacy Policy

Transformix LLC (hereinafter "the Company") recognizes the protection of personal information as an important responsibility and handles it appropriately in accordance with the following policy.

1. Collection of Personal Information

The Company may collect personal information such as name, company name, email address, and inquiry content through contact forms and other means. Personal information is collected through lawful and fair means.

2. Purpose of Use

Personal information collected will be used for the following purposes:

  1. Responding to inquiries
  2. Proposing and providing services
  3. Executing contracts and processing payments
  4. Other purposes incidental to the above

3. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company will not provide personal information to third parties except in the following cases:

  1. When consent has been obtained from the individual
  2. When required by law
  3. When necessary to protect the life, body, or property of a person

4. Outsourcing

The Company may outsource the handling of personal information to third parties within the scope necessary to achieve the purposes of use. In such cases, appropriate supervision will be exercised over the outsourced party.

5. Security Management

The Company takes appropriate security measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration, or leakage of personal information.

6. Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Individuals may request disclosure, correction, deletion, or suspension of use of their personal information held by the Company. Please contact us at the address below. After verifying your identity, we will respond within a reasonable period.

The Company's website may use cookies and access analytics tools to improve services. These tools do not collect personally identifiable information.

8. Amendments to This Policy

The Company may amend this policy in response to changes in laws or service content. Any amendments will be announced on this website.
